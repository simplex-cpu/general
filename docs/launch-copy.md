## Landing page (risevoicetools.com)

**Navigation:** How it works · Plans · FAQ · Log in · **Start free trial**

**Hero**
- Eyebrow: For coaches, consultants and creators
- Headline: A week of content, ready to approve.
- Subheadline: Risevoice turns your brand into a content plan, writes your scripts and films them with your AI avatar. You approve the finished videos and post.
- Buttons: **Start free trial** · Get it done for you
- Small print: Free trial · No card needed · Prefer to film yourself? That works too.
- Image: the Planner screenshot (`public/landing/planner.jpg`)

**Proof line:** Built and tested on the kashsocialmedia tips account. *(Add week-1 results after 12 November.)*

**How it works**
1. **Strategy.** Set your goals and get your content mix and topics.
2. **Your brand.** Drop in your website or brand kit: colours, fonts, tone.
3. **Your avatar.** Record once. Your AI avatar films your talking videos.
4. **Scripts.** Write them with AI, or switch on Autopilot for a full week every Monday.
5. **Approve.** Watch the finished videos, approve, schedule.

**Feature: no filming**
- Label: No filming
- Title: Send any script to your AI avatar
- Text: One click turns an approved script into a finished talking video. It comes back to your Planner ready to approve.
- Image: `public/landing/send-to-avatar.jpg`

**Feature: your voice**
- Label: Your voice
- Title: Refine any script in one click
- Text: Shorter, punchier, more like you. Change one script without starting again.
- Image: `public/landing/refine.jpg`

**Plans** *(no prices until they're set)*
- **Creator.** The tool. Strategy, scripts and Autopilot · Your AI avatar films your talking videos · Your brand on every design · Planner to approve and schedule. Button: **Start free trial**
- **Done for you.** The tool and your own account manager, by application. Everything in Creator · Your account manager plans, scripts and produces your week · You only approve finished videos · Posting handled for you. Button: **Apply for done for you**

**Questions**
- **Do I have to film?** No. Your AI avatar films your talking videos. You can still film yourself whenever you want.
- **How is my avatar made?** You record a short video once, with your consent. Only your account can use it.
- **What does the account manager do?** Plans your week, writes and refines scripts, sends videos to production and posts. You approve.
- **What happens after the trial?** Choose Creator, or apply for Done for you.

**Closing band**
- Headline: Your next week of content starts here.
- Text: Or comment CREATORS on our latest post and we'll send you the link.
- Button: **Start free trial**
